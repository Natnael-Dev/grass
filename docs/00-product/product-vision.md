# Product Vision — Grass

**Purpose:** define what Grass is, who it's for, and the principles that settle disagreements. **Read in:** M0.

## One sentence
Grass is a tiny website that puts a calm, living meadow in your browser so a developer can take a one-minute break from the screen.

## Value proposition
"touch grass" is a joke developers hear constantly. Grass makes it literal, beautiful and shareable: open the page, click once, and you are standing in a field that responds to you. It costs nothing, needs no account, and works in seconds.

## Audience
Primary: programmers and people who work at screens (desktop browser, mid-session). Secondary: anyone who receives the link from a friend, often on a phone.

## Personality and tone
Calm and atmospheric with dry, kind developer humor. Lowercase, terminal-flavored microcopy (`> hold the mouse down to lie down. you've earned it.`). The grass is the star; the humor is a seasoning. Never preachy, never a "wellness app", no productivity guilt.

## Differentiation
Not a generic 3D scene, not a meditation site. Distinct through: (1) *touch that feels good* (grass parts, springs back with overshoot), (2) *lie down* (camera dips into the grass), (3) *dev voice* in small doses, (4) three carefully art-directed moods on one hill, (5) tiny and instant. It must **not** imitate the layout, copy, assets or wording of any existing "touch grass" site.

## The shareable moment
Lying in the grass at night with fireflies while the dev-voiced line appears. (Optional OE-04 adds "save wallpaper".)

## Product principles (tie-breakers, in priority order)
1. **Immediate delight:** the first interaction must feel good within 5 seconds of arriving.
2. **Smooth over spectacular:** a stable frame rate beats extra effects. Cut effects before dropping frames.
3. **The environment is the interface:** minimal UI, nothing between the person and the grass.
4. **Small and polished:** fewer mechanics, each excellent. Reject feature creep.
5. **Kind humor, sparingly:** one good line beats five.
6. **Respect the visitor:** no tracking, no accounts, no nagging, works offline-ish once loaded, honors reduced-motion and mute.

## User experience in one pass
Open page → static entry overlay paints instantly with title and the "step outside" button while the scene prepares behind it → click → overlay fades, camera eases in, audio fades up → hint line guides: brush, click, hold → user explores moods → eventually closes the tab or types `exit`. Full detail: `user-journeys.md`.

## Success (qualitative; no analytics)
A developer smiles within 10 seconds, uses all three interactions without reading anything but the hint line, and wants to send it to someone. Verified by the human at gates.
