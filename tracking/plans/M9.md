## 1. Objectives & Scope

- **Web Audio Lifecycle & Autoplay Compliance:**
  - AudioContext is created/unlocked **strictly** on user entry interaction (clicking `#btn-enter` or pressing Enter). Zero audio runs beforehand.
  - Also resumes on first `touchend` for iOS.
  - Graceful fallback: If `AudioContext` is unsupported or fails, app continues silently with zero errors and hides the sound toggle button.
  - Master gain default: 0.5. Mute ramps over 300 ms (`linearRampToValueAtTime`). Persisted in `grass:muted`.
  - `visibilitychange` hidden $\rightarrow$ `ctx.suspend()`; visible $\rightarrow$ `ctx.resume()` (if unmuted).
- **Audio Graph Topology:**
  - `layers → ambienceBus (lowpass filter: 18 kHz default, gain) → masterGain → compressor → destination`
  - `sfxBus (gain) → masterGain` (bypasses ambience lowpass filter)
  - `compressor`: light DynamicsCompressorNode (threshold -12 dB, knee 10 dB, ratio 3, attack 0.01s, release 0.25s) to prevent any clipping.
- **Procedural Synthesizers (`src/audio/`):**
  - **Wind (`Wind.ts`)**:
    - 4 s looping pink-noise AudioBuffer.
    - Bandpass filter ($f_0 = 420\text{ Hz}$, $Q = 0.7$).
    - 0.07 Hz LFO modulating cutoff $\pm 160\text{ Hz}$ and gain 0.5–1.0.
    - Highpassed 1.2 kHz "hiss" layer at low gain.
    - Control formula: $\text{gain} = \text{mood.audioWind} \times (0.6 + 0.4 \times \text{windStrength})$.
  - **Rustle (`Rustle.ts`)**:
    - Brush-driven short noise grains (60–140 ms).
    - Bandpass filter (3.2 kHz, $Q = 1.2$).
    - Random stereo pan $\pm 0.4$.
    - Rate proportional to brush speed ($\le 12\text{ grains/s}$ max), gain proportional to strength. Zero grains when stationary.
  - **Crickets (`Crickets.ts`)**:
    - 3 detuned sine voices (4.1 kHz, 4.35 kHz, 4.6 kHz).
    - 28 Hz amplitude modulation grouped in chirps of 3 every 0.7–1.3 s (randomized).
    - Very low gain 0.02–0.035.
    - Control: $\text{gain} = \text{mood.audioCrickets}$ (high at night, zero at dawn/golden).
  - **Birds (`Birds.ts`)**:
    - Scheduled chirps every 2–7 s: sine sweep 3200 $\rightarrow$ 4800 Hz in 70–120 ms with 25 Hz vibrato, 1–3 syllables, random stereo pan.
    - Control: $\text{gain} = \text{mood.audioBirds}$ (high at dawn 0.9, golden 0.6, zero at night).
  - **SFX (`Sfx.ts`)**:
    - `playSeedScatter()`: 140 ms noise "pff" (bandpass 1.8 kHz) + soft sine blip 520 $\rightarrow$ 680 Hz (90 ms).
  - **Lie-down Ambience Filter**:
    - Ambience lowpass cutoff ramps from 18 kHz $\rightarrow$ 2.2 kHz over 1.1 s, wind gain $\times 1.25$ (cosy, muffled, closer).
    - Reverses smoothly back to 18 kHz / 1.0x on release over 1.1 s.
- **Verification & Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Pure function unit tests for scheduler, noise generation, and gain mapping formulas.
  - Vitest audio suite with Web Audio mocks.
  - Playwright E2E tests:
    - Silent before entry (no running context).
    - Running after entry.
    - Mute button ramps gain to 0 and updates `aria-pressed`.
    - Tab background/visibilitychange suspends and resumes AudioContext.
    - Keyboard <kbd>M</kbd> toggles mute.
    - Mood transitions smoothly update layer gains.
