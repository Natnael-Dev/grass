# Milestone M7: Seeds and Fireflies (Gate G6) Implementation Plan

## Goal

Implement the tactile dandelion seeds scattering interaction and serene nighttime fireflies:

1. Procedural 6-spoke fluffy dandelion seed texture generated on offscreen canvas.
2. `Seeds` particle system:
   - CPU-side typed-array simulation (positions, velocities, ages, lifetimes, alphas, sizes).
   - Tier spawn counts: 18 (high), 14 (medium), 10 (low).
   - Physical motion: upward launch 1.2–2.4 m/s, random horizontal 0.3–0.8 m/s, wind acceleration $0.6 \cdot \text{windStrength}$, linear drag $0.8/\text{s}$, sink $0.25\text{ m/s}$ after apex.
   - Lifetime: 3.5–5.5 s with 0.15 s fade-in and 1.0 s fade-out.
   - Tinted by mood `lightColor`.
   - Coupled tap brush impulse: radius $\times 1.3$, strength $1.0$.
3. `Fireflies` particle system:
   - GPU-animated `Points` with custom `ShaderMaterial`.
   - Tier counts: 90 (high), 60 (medium), 30 (low).
   - Distributed in meadow volume in front of camera ($x \in [-35, 35]$, $z \in [-30, 15]$, $y \in [0.2, 2.2]\text{ m}$ above terrain).
   - Continuous Lissajous drift in vertex shader.
   - Soft blinking additive glow with `#d9ff7a` color and radial falloff.
   - Visibility and opacity smoothly governed by `moodState.firefliesAmount` (1 at night, 0 at dawn/golden).
4. Memory & Performance:
   - Zero-allocation hot path during updates.
   - Dynamic draw call optimization: seeds hidden when active count is 0; fireflies hidden during daytime.
   - Total scene draw calls stay $\le 9$ (well under $\le 12$ budget).
5. Comprehensive unit tests and Playwright E2E tests verifying lifecycle, budgets, and visual screenshots.
