## 1. Objectives & Scope

- **Responsive Viewport & Safe-Area Layouts (`index.html`, `tokens.css`):**
  - Remove `user-scalable=no` (WCAG compliance); keep `viewport-fit=cover`.
  - Use `100dvh` for full-screen layers; adhere to safe area insets: `env(safe-area-inset-top)`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, `env(safe-area-inset-left)`, `env(safe-area-inset-right)`.
  - Landmark structure: `<main id="app">` wrapping scene; `<nav aria-label="controls">` for HUD.
  - Narrow/portrait (< 640 px):
    - Camera FOV expands to 55° in portrait to preserve meadow and tree visibility.
    - Mood pills remain bottom-centered with $\ge 44\text{ px}$ touch targets.
    - Top controls (sound + help) inset with safe areas.
    - Uptime counter relocates above mood pills centered.
    - Hint line wraps to 2 lines max with width $\le 90\text{vw}$.
  - Small height (< 420 px landscape phone):
    - Hide uptime counter, shrink mood pills to icons-only.
- **Touch Interaction & Heuristics:**
  - Touch detection in `capabilities.ts`: defaults tier to `medium` on touch devices if no explicit `?quality=` override.
  - Hand cursor hidden on touch devices (`isTouchOrPen`).
  - Contextual touch hints: `drag to brush the grass`, `tap anywhere to scatter seeds`, `press and hold to lie down in the meadow`.
  - Prevent default browser behaviors on canvas only: `touch-action: none` and `overscroll-behavior: none`.
- **Reduced Motion Support (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):**
  - Dynamic live-updating via `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` change listener.
  - Wind amplitude scaled by $\times 0.35$ (`uReduced` in `grass.vert.glsl`).
  - Fireflies: steady glow (no blinking, drift scaled by $\times 0.3$).
  - CameraRig: no parallax drift, no idle sway or gust, lie-down 300 ms linear with no FOV or vignette change.
  - Mood crossfade shortened to 1.0 s.
  - HintLine: instant typed text rendering.
  - HandCursor: zero rotational tilt.
- **High Contrast / Forced Colors (`@media (forced-colors: active)`):**
  - System colors: `Canvas`, `CanvasText`, `ButtonBorder`, `Highlight`.
  - Glass panels and interactive controls gain high-contrast borders and clear focus outlines.
- **WebGL Fallback & Context Loss Recovery:**
  - `webglcontextlost` event listener with `e.preventDefault()`.
  - Displays recovery toast: `the meadow blinked. recovering…`.
  - If `webglcontextrestored` fires, seamlessly recovers or reloads.
  - If not restored within 5.0 s, transitions to full fallback overlay with manual reload action.
- **WCAG AA Contrast Verification:**
  - Color contrast script `scripts/check-contrast.mjs` verifying $\ge 4.5:1$ on text against panel and brightest horizon across all moods.
  - Unit test `tests/unit/a11y-contrast.spec.ts`.
- **E2E Playwright Suite (`tests/e2e/mobile-a11y.spec.ts`):**
  - iPhone 13 & Pixel 5 portrait and landscape emulations with screenshot evidence.
  - Reduced motion and forced colors emulation tests.
  - Context loss simulation test.
  - Lighthouse / axe accessibility assertions (score $\ge 95$).
